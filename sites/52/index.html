Top 10 Horror/Disturbing Books (According to me)


10. Sweetpea by C.J. Skuse


Rhiannon Lewis looks like an ordinary girl living a quiet life with her boyfriend and dog,but she secretly keeps a "kill list" of people who annoy her.When a real emergency pushes her over the edge, she unleashes her inner serial killer, managing to commit greusome murders while maintaining her sweet, innocent farcade.



9. Horrorstör by Grady Hendrix

Employees volunteering for an overnight shift at an IKEA-style furniture store discover it was built on a brutal 19th-century prison.At night, the sadistic warden's ghost returns to subject the staff to horrific, corporate-themed torture.



8. The Shining Girls by Lauren Beukes


Harper Curtis is a time-travelling serial killer in Depression-era Chicago who must murder "Shining girls"-bright yound women with glowing potential-tokeep his portal open. His final target, Kirby Mazrachi,survives his brutal attack and dedicates her life to hunting him down across different decades.



7. The Ruins by Scott Smith


Four American tourists and a Greek traveler visit a remote, vine-covered Mayan ruin in the Mexican jungle, only to be trapped there by armed villagers.They quickly discoves the sentient, carnivorus vines covering the hill can mimic human sounds and will brutallyt consume them alive one by one



6. Baby Teeth by Zoje Stage


Suzette Landy loves her sweet husband but faces a living nightmare with their mute seven-year-old daughter, Hanna, who is secretly a manipulative sociopath. Hanna is completely obsessed with her father and launches a covert, escalating campaign of psychological and physical terror to eliminate her mother permanently



5. The Raft by Stephen King


Four college students swim out to a wooden raft on a secluded lake, only to be trapped by a sentient, flesh-eating black slime floating in the water. The creature patiently circles the raft, brutally dissolving and consuming them one by one the moment they touch the water or slip through the cracks



4. Devolution by Max Brooks


Following the eruption of Mount Rainier, the residents of a high-tech, eco-friendly community are cut off from civilization and left completely isolated. They are forced to fight for survival against a pack of displaced, highly aggressive, and fiercely intelligent Sasquatches (Bigfoots) hunting them down.



3. Come Closer by Sara Gran


Amanda is a successful architect who begins experiencing subtle, disturbing personality changes, like tapping her teeth, stealing, and having cruel thoughts. She slowly discovers she is being targeted and systematically possessed by a beautiful, ancient female demon named Naamah.



2. The Troop by Nick Cutter


A Scoutmaster takes a group of five young Boy Scouts on a weekend camping trip to a remote Canadian island. Their survival weekend turns into a gory nightmare when a bioengineered, insatiable tapeworm gets loose, infecting them and driving the boys to horrific extremes of hunger and violence.



1. Tender Is the Flesh by Agustina Bazterrica


After a sudden virus renders all animal meat poisonous, governments legalize the breeding and slaughter of humans—referred to as "special meat"—for consumption. A grieving slaughterhouse manager is gifted a live, high-quality human specimen, sparking a dangerous emotional conflict as he breaks the law by treating her as a human being.
